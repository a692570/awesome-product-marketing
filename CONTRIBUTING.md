# Contributing to Awesome Product Marketing

Thanks for considering a contribution. This list is opinionated on purpose. The bar is not "exists and is about PMM" but "I would send this to a PMM who is about to make a mistake without it."

## Before you open a PR

1. **Search the list first.** Duplicate suggestions get closed. If your resource covers the same topic as an existing entry, your PR needs to explain what the new one adds that the old one does not.
2. **Check the link works.** Open it in an incognito window. If it hits a paywall, 404s, redirects to a different URL, or requires login to view the core content, it does not belong here.
3. **Read the existing tone.** Each entry has a one-line description that says what the resource is and why a PMM should care. Match that format. No marketing copy, no "comprehensive guide to unlocking potential."

## What we accept

- Articles, books, videos, tools, newsletters, and communities useful to PMMs at high-growth or developer-focused companies.
- Practitioner-written content over vendor content. A blog post by someone who shipped the thing beats a vendor whitepaper about the thing.
- Resources published or updated in the last 2 years, unless it is a foundational text (April Dunford, Geoffrey Moore, etc.) that has not been superseded.
- Developer marketing and DevRel resources. This list leans toward dev-focused PMM work, not generic B2B marketing.

## What we do not accept

- Your company's blog post about your product. Vendor content about its own category is fine. Vendor content about itself is not.
- Affiliate link farms, SEO content mills, or anything that exists to rank rather than to teach.
- Generic "what is product marketing" listicles with no practitioner insight.
- Resources behind hard paywalls with no free preview. Soft gates (email required) are fine if the content delivers.
- Duplicative content. If the same idea is already covered by a better source in the list, the new one does not get in just because it exists.

## How to submit

1. Fork the repo.
2. Add your entry to the correct section in `README.md`. Use this format:

```
- [Title of Resource](https://example.com) - Author or Source
  One line on what it is. One line on why a PMM should care.
```

3. In your PR description, answer three questions:
   - **Working link?** Confirm you tested it in an incognito window.
   - **Why is this resource good?** What does it teach or do that the existing entries in that section do not? Be specific. "It is comprehensive" is not an answer.
   - **Which section and why?** If it could fit in two sections, pick one and explain the choice.
4. Open the PR with the title: `Add: [Resource Title]`.

## Review process

PRs are reviewed within 7 days. If a PR is not accepted, you will get a reason. If it is accepted, it may be edited for formatting or tone before merge.

## Maintenance

Dead links are audited periodically. If you notice a link is broken, open an issue or a PR with the fix or the removal.
