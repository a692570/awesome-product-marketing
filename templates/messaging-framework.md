## Buying committee engagement (extend for enterprise)

| Role | Content goal | Content type | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision-maker | Impact on core metric | Executive summary + benchmark | Direct + ABM ad |
| Champion | Equip to sell internally | Business case template + comparison doc | Direct + email |
| Blocker | Convert, not bypass | Career progression content | Direct + retargeting |
| Influencer | Reduce perceived risk | Architecture docs + compliance matrix | Docs site + retargeting |

### Opp-stage multi-threading

When an opp hits stage 1-2, trigger ads to the full buying group. The ads help the AE multi-thread without cold-asking the champion to bring in their boss. Measure by win rate of multi-threaded vs single-threaded opps, not ad CTR.
