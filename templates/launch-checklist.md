# Product Launch Checklist

> A structured launch checklist for dev-focused and AI-era products.
> Adapted from work by Abhishek Sharma (PMM, Telnyx) and the FullFunnel B2B Marketing playbook.

## How to use this template

Work through each phase in order. Do not skip the pre-launch gates. A launch that ships without positioning clarity wastes the moment.

---

## Phase 0: Positioning (do this first)

- [ ] Positioning doc completed (see `positioning-doc.md`)
- [ ] Messaging hierarchy approved by PM and sales lead
- [ ] Competitive alternatives documented honestly (including "do nothing")
- [ ] Enterprise buying realities checked (Math vs Drama, champion gates)
- [ ] Account-level POV drafted for top 5 target accounts (if ABM)

## Phase 1: Pre-launch (2-4 weeks before)

### Assets
- [ ] Launch blog post drafted
- [ ] Product docs updated
- [ ] API reference updated (if applicable)
- [ ] Landing page live (or staged, ready to flip)
- [ ] Demo video or walkthrough recorded
- [ ] Social graphics created (3-5 platform-ready variants)
- [ ] Battle card updated for sales team
- [ ] Two-pager updated for sales team
- [ ] Email sequence drafted (announcement, follow-up, nurture)

### Internal readiness
- [ ] Sales team briefed on positioning and messaging
- [ ] Support team briefed on new features and known issues
- [ ] Customer success team briefed on what existing customers get
- [ ] Launch date confirmed with engineering (feature is merged or staging-ready)
- [ ] Press/analyst briefings scheduled (if applicable)

### Technical readiness
- [ ] Landing page passes agent readability check (llms.txt, text/plain, no JS-only content)
- [ ] Docs are crawlable and indexable
- [ ] Sitemap updated
- [ ] AEO/GEO basics checked (structured data, clear Q/A format on landing page)

## Phase 2: Launch day

### Announcement sequence
- [ ] Blog post published
- [ ] Product Hunt launch (if dev-focused)
- [ ] X/Twitter announcement thread posted
- [ ] LinkedIn announcement posted
- [ ] Email blast sent to subscriber list
- [ ] Community post (Discord, Slack, Reddit, HackerNews)
- [ ] Dev newsletter sponsorship activated (if planned)

### Internal
- [ ] Sales team available for inbound questions
- [ ] Support team staffed for increased volume
- [ ] Engineering on-call for launch-day issues

## Phase 3: Post-launch (week 1)

- [ ] Monitor signups, demos, and trials
- [ ] Collect early feedback from first users
- [ ] Track social engagement and respond to comments
- [ ] Identify top 3 customer quotes for social proof
- [ ] Post-launch recap blog post or thread (what we learned)
- [ ] Update internal wiki with launch results

## Phase 4: Sustain (weeks 2-4)

- [ ] Content expansion: turn the launch into 3-5 derivative pieces (deep-dive blog, tutorial, comparison post, video)
- [ ] Social-first validation: post key angles on LinkedIn, measure 48-hour engagement, expand winners to long-form
- [ ] Customer story collection: interview first 2-3 adopters
- [ ] Battle card refinement based on real competitive encounters
- [ ] Metrics review: signups, activation rate, demo conversion, pipeline influenced

## Launch video (optional, recommended for dev products)

- [ ] Script written (focus on the 10-second hook, not the full feature tour)
- [ ] Recorded with HyperFrames or Remotion (code-as-video, no timeline editor needed)
- [ ] 15-30 second version for social
- [ ] 60-90 second version for landing page
- [ ] Captioned and accessible
