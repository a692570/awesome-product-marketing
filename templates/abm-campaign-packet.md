# ABM Campaign Packet Template

> A structured campaign packet for account-based marketing at companies with long sales cycles.
> Based on the FullFunnel B2B Marketing playbook (Andrei Zinkevich) and the Telnyx ABM Campaign Engine.
> Adapted by Abhishek Sharma (PMM, Telnyx).

## How to use this template

Build the packet in order. Each section feeds the next. Do not skip the evidence packet and go straight to content ideas. A campaign without evidence is a guess.

| List | Criteria | Count | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
