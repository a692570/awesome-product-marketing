# Positioning Doc Template

> Based on the April Dunford framework, extended with enterprise buying realities (Math vs Drama, champion reality check, 400+ touchpoints).
> Adapted from work by Abhishek Sharma (PMM, Telnyx) and the FullFunnel B2B Marketing playbook.

## How to use this template

Fill in each section. Do not skip steps. The framework is sequential: each answer feeds the next. If you cannot answer a section, you are not ready to position this product.

---

## 1. Competitive alternatives

What would the target customer do if your product did not exist? Include "do nothing" and "keep current vendor" as real alternatives, not strawmen.

| Alternative | What they get | What they give up |
|---|---|---|
| Do nothing | No spend, no change | Problem persists, competitor gains |
| Current vendor | Familiarity, existing relationships | Missing capability X |
| Competitor A | Feature Y | Higher cost, weaker Z |
| Competitor B | Market presence | Longer onboarding |
