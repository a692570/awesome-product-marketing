# Templates

Ready-to-use PMM templates from Abhishek Sharma (PMM, Telnyx). These are built on real workflows, not theoretical frameworks. Each template integrates methods from the [FullFunnel B2B Marketing playbook](https://fullfunnel.substack.com) (Andrei Zinkevich) and practical positioning work at Telnyx.

## Templates

| Template | What it produces | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| [positioning-doc.md](positioning-doc.md) | A complete positioning document | Before any launch, content, or sales enablement work |
| [launch-checklist.md](launch-checklist.md) | A structured launch plan with pre/launch/post phases | When shipping a new product or feature |
| [abm-campaign-packet.md](abm-campaign-packet.md) | A full ABM campaign packet with account prioritization, signal strategy, and measurement | When building an account-based marketing motion |
| [battle-card.md](battle-card.md) | A one-page competitive battle card for sales reps | When reps need ammunition against a specific competitor |
| [messaging-framework.md](messaging-framework.md) | A 10-section messaging reference doc | When the team needs a single source of truth for product messaging |
| [content-angles.md](content-angles.md) | Differentiated content angles that pass a quality gate | When planning content and need angles that stand out |

## How these were built

Each template draws from:

- **April Dunford's positioning framework** (Obviously Awesome), extended with enterprise buying realities
- **FullFunnel B2B Marketing** (Andrei Zinkevich): cluster targeting, 3-list dynamic prioritization, 6A account journey, signal-based strategy, ABM sprint launch, measurement framework, buying committee engagement
- **Telnyx PMM practice**: real sales enablement, competitive positioning, and content angle work at a developer-focused infrastructure company
